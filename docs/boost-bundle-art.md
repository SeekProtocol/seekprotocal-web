- A unified composition replaces the three overlapping image tiles. No catalog or fulfillment data changed.

## Composition prompt

Use case: compositing. Asset type: a single polished game shop bundle illustration, square 1024px, displayed as a small product thumbnail. Input references: image 1 is the Shiba astronaut sitting on a golden crescent moon, image 2 is the red horseshoe coin magnet with gold coins, image 3 is the cheerful gold coin superhero riding a white and red rocket. Preserve these three recognizable designs and their glossy sculpted collectible art style. Replace the stacked square icon tiles with ONE unified, carefully art-directed illustration: the Shiba and crescent on the left, the coin magnet in the upper right, the coin rider and rocket in the lower right. All three subjects equally legible, their main silhouettes, faces and distinguishing features fully visible, separated by generous breathing room, minimal overlap. Cohesive warm gold highlights and subtle cool blue rim light. Truly transparent background with alpha; no rectangular backgrounds, no cards, no border, no box, no platform, no labels or lettering, no extra characters. Keep effects restrained, only a small rocket flame and two coins near the magnet. Compact balanced triangular composition occupying the central 88 percent of the square, clear outer silhouette, optimized to remain readable at 148px height.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit this bundle illustration. Keep all three collectible subjects, their identities, poses, glossy materials, relative placement and appearance unchanged. Replace the ENTIRE gray checkerboard background with a perfectly smooth flat dark midnight background, hex #0b0d15, edge to edge. Remove every trace of checkerboard, every wrinkle and all fabric texture. No transparency requested. The background must be a calm smooth near-black studio backdrop matching premium game item icons. Give the complete group a little more breathing room from the canvas edges, preserving the three subjects. No cards, tiles, frames, labels or new objects. Square final image.

The first transparency attempt produced a visible checkerboard and was discarded. The final image uses a smooth dark background to match the individual booster illustrations.
